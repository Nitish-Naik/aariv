# 🔐 Privacy Policy — What to Include

## 🎯 Guiding Principle

> Collect **as little as possible**, explain it **clearly**, and give users **control**.

---

## 1️⃣ What Data You Collect (Be Explicit & Minimal)

### Required

* Google account basic profile (name, email, avatar)
* Authentication identifiers (Supabase / OAuth IDs)

### Optional (only after user connects)

* Email metadata (sender, subject, timestamp)
* Calendar metadata (event title, time)
* App connection status (which apps are connected)

✅ State clearly:

* **No passwords**
* **No email content stored permanently unless required**
* **No silent connections**

**Example copy**

```
We collect only the information needed to sign you in and provide features you explicitly enable.
```

---

## 2️⃣ What You Do NOT Collect (This Builds Trust)

Explicitly list this — it matters.

```
We do NOT:
• Read or modify your emails without asking
• Store email bodies permanently
• Access private files or photos
• Sell or share your data
• Train AI models on your personal content
```

---

## 3️⃣ How Integrations Work (Composio Clarity)

Explain this simply, without naming vendors unless necessary.

```
When you connect an app (like Gmail or Calendar), access is granted securely through an industry-standard provider. You can disconnect at any time.
```

Optional transparency:

```
Third-party integrations are SOC2-compliant.
```

---

## 4️⃣ User Control & Consent

Must include:

* Users choose what to connect
* Users can revoke access anytime
* Users can delete their account

```
You are always in control of your data. You can disconnect apps or delete your account at any time from settings.
```

---

## 5️⃣ Data Deletion Policy (Critical)

This must be crystal clear.

```
When you delete your account:
• All user data is permanently removed
• All integrations are revoked
• Active sessions are invalidated
```

---

## 6️⃣ Security Measures (High-Level Only)

Don’t go technical.

```
We use encryption, secure authentication, and access controls to protect your data.
```

---

## 7️⃣ Contact & Responsibility

```
If you have questions or concerns, contact us at: support@aariv.app
```

# 🚫 What to Avoid (Very Important)

❌ Avoid:

* Long legal paragraphs
* Scare language (“we reserve the right…”) everywhere
* Vague “we may collect data” statements
* AI training clauses (unless you really do that)

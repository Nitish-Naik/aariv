# Getting Started with CalmPilot

Welcome. This guide walks you through signing up, connecting your first app, and getting your first morning briefing — start to finish in about 3 minutes.

---

## What is CalmPilot?

CalmPilot is an AI assistant that connects to your work apps (Gmail, Slack, Calendar, GitHub, Notion, and more) and handles routine tasks for you. Wake up to a morning brief with everything summarized — no more checking 5 apps before coffee. Instead of you chasing your inbox, CalmPilot watches it for you and tells you only what actually needs your attention.

---

## Step 1: Sign Up

1. Go to [calmpilot.app](https://calmpilot.app) and click **Get Started Free**.
2. Click **Continue with Google** — CalmPilot uses your Google account to sign you in. No password to create.
3. You'll see a welcome screen with your first name. You're in.

That's it. Your account is created. You start with $5 of free credits — enough to explore the product fully before deciding whether to subscribe.

> **No credit card required.** You won't be charged anything until you choose a plan.

---

## Step 2: Connect Your First App

After signing up, CalmPilot will guide you to connect Gmail. This is the most important first step — once Gmail is connected, CalmPilot can read your inbox and generate a real briefing based on your actual emails.

1. On the welcome screen, click **Connect Gmail**.
2. A Google sign-in popup will appear. Choose the Google account you use for work.
3. Grant the permissions CalmPilot requests — these allow it to read your inbox and calendar on your behalf.
4. Once connected, CalmPilot will ask if you also want to connect **Google Calendar** (same account, one extra click). We recommend saying yes — it makes your briefings much richer.

**Why Gmail first?** Together, Gmail and Google Calendar cover the majority of what shows up in your morning briefing. They're the fastest path to seeing CalmPilot actually work.

---

## Step 3: Watch CalmPilot Set Up

After connecting, you'll see a "Setting up Aariv for you..." screen. This takes about 30 to 60 seconds. CalmPilot is:

- Activating monitoring on your connected apps
- Reading your last 10 emails to populate your first brief
- Preparing your briefing

You'll see live progress as each step completes. When it's done, you're taken straight to your first briefing.

---

## Step 4: Your First Morning Briefing

Your dashboard will load with a briefing built from your actual data. It might look something like this:

> **Good morning, Alex.**
> Here's what needs you today.
>
> - 3 emails need attention
> - 2 meetings today (Design review at 2 PM, 1:1 at 4 PM)
> - Aariv noticed: You have 3.5 hours of focus time before your first meeting.

This is real data from your inbox and calendar — not a demo. If you only connected Gmail, you'll see email items. If you also connected Calendar, you'll see your meetings too.

From this point on, every time you open CalmPilot, your briefing is waiting for you.

---

## Step 5: Ask the AI a Question

After your briefing, try talking to Aariv (the AI inside CalmPilot). Click **Ask Aariv** from the dashboard, or go to the **Assistant** tab.

Type something like:

- *"What's on my calendar today?"*
- *"Summarize my unread emails."*
- *"Draft a reply to the last email from my manager."*

Aariv will check your connected apps and respond — and actually take action if you ask it to. See the [Assistant guide](./assistant.md) for more examples and tips.

---

## Understanding Credits

CalmPilot uses a credit system. Each time the AI does something — generates a briefing, answers a question, sends an email — a small number of credits are used.

You start with **$5 of free credits**. Once those run out, you can subscribe to a plan to keep going.

Credits are designed to be inexpensive. A typical morning briefing costs less than a fraction of a cent. Normal daily use should cost well under $1/month on a standard plan.

> For full details on pricing and plans, see the [Billing guide](./billing.md).

# Plans and Billing

## How It Works

CalmPilot uses a subscription model with three tiers: Free, Starter, and Pro.

Your plan determines how many chat messages you get per month and how many triggers you can run. Background automation — triggers watching your apps and generating your morning briefing — is included in paid plans and never drains your chat allowance.

For full pricing details, see the [Pricing Strategy](../../PRICING_STRATEGY.md) document or visit the [Pricing page](https://calmpilot.app/pricing) in the app.

## What Uses Your Monthly Messages

**Chat messages** are used when you talk to the AI assistant. Every message you send counts as one.

**Morning briefings** on paid plans do not count against your chat messages — they run automatically in the background using the automation budget included in your subscription.

**Trigger processing** also does not count against your chat messages. CalmPilot watches your apps continuously on paid plans without touching your message allowance.

In short: your chat message count is only for your direct conversations with the assistant.

---

## Checking Your Usage

Go to **Settings** in the sidebar and select **Plan and Billing**. You will see:

- Your current plan
- How many chat messages you have used this month
- How many messages remain
- When your monthly count resets
- Options to upgrade or manage your subscription

---

## Upgrading Your Plan

1. Go to **Settings** in the sidebar.
2. Click on **Plan and Billing**.
3. Click **Upgrade** next to the plan you want.
4. You will be taken to the checkout page — enter your card details.
5. Your plan upgrades immediately on payment.

You can also go directly to the [Pricing page](https://calmpilot.app/pricing) and click the plan you want.

---

## What You Get on the Free Plan

When you sign up, you are on the Free plan. Here is exactly what that includes:

- **50 chat messages per month** — enough to explore the product and get a feel for what the AI can do
- **3 active triggers** — connect up to 3 apps and have them monitored
- **10 trigger events per day** — CalmPilot will process up to 10 background events daily
- **No morning briefing** — the daily briefing is a paid feature

The Free plan is designed to let you see how CalmPilot works before committing. Most people hit the limits within a week or two of regular use.

---

## When to Upgrade

**Upgrade to Starter ($19/month) if you:**
- Want a daily morning briefing
- Need more than 3 triggers running at once
- Use the chat assistant regularly and keep hitting the 50-message limit
- Want a noticeably better AI model for answers and actions

**Upgrade to Pro ($49/month) if you:**
- Want the most powerful AI model available
- Send more than 500 chat messages per month
- Want priority briefing processing

---

## Running Out of Messages

If you reach your monthly chat message limit:
- The AI assistant will stop responding to new messages.
- A prompt will appear asking you to upgrade or wait for your monthly reset.
- Your triggers keep running and your background automation continues — only chat pauses.
- You can send extra messages at $0.03 each (Starter) or $0.02 each (Pro) without upgrading.

Your message count resets at the start of each billing cycle. Unused messages do not roll over.

---

## Cancelling

To cancel your subscription, go to **Settings > Plan and Billing** and select **Cancel Plan**. You will keep access to your paid features until the end of your current billing period, then your account will move to the Free tier.

# Pinned Tweet Strategy

## Current pin (has link — kills reach):
"I built an AI that reads my Gmail overnight... Free to try → calmpilot.app"

## DON'T change the pin right now.

The pinned tweet is NOT distributed by the algorithm — it only shows when people visit your profile.
Since it's not in the feed, the link penalty doesn't apply.

The link IN the pin is actually GOOD because:
- People who visit your profile are already interested
- They need a direct CTA to convert
- The calmpilot.app card preview adds visual credibility

## When to change the pin:
- When the demo video tweet gets 500+ views → pin that instead
- When you have a testimonial → pin that
- When you launch on Product Hunt → pin the launch tweet

## Rule:
- Feed tweets: NO links (algorithm penalty)
- Pinned tweet: YES links (profile visitors need a CTA)
- Bio: YES link (calmpilot.app is already there)

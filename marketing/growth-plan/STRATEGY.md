## Growth Formula
**Replies (discovery) + Pain-point content (attraction) + Demo (conversion) + Consistency (compounding)**

## Daily Routine (30-45 min/day)

### Morning (15 min)
1. Open X notifications — reply to anyone who engaged
2. Find 5 big accounts posting about: productivity, AI tools, email overload, automation, indie hacking
3. Reply to their posts (add value, don't pitch)

### Afternoon (15 min)
4. Post your scheduled tweet for the day
5. Find 5 more accounts to reply to (different niche: founders, remote workers, PMs)

### Evening (10 min)
6. Post second tweet if scheduled
7. Reply to any new comments on your posts
8. Check what content performed — note patterns

## Reply Formula
1. **Agree or disagree** with a specific point (not "great post!")
2. **Add something** from your experience building CalmPilot
3. **Only mention product** if directly relevant (max 1 in 5 replies)
4. **Keep it under 280 chars** — punchy > long

## Content Principles
1. Lead with the PROBLEM, not the solution
2. Numbers > adjectives ("40 min/day" > "too much time")
3. Controversy > consensus (hot takes get shared)
4. Show, don't tell (screenshots, GIFs, real data)
5. Never beg for follows/likes — earn them with value

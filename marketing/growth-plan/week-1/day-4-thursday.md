# Day 4 - Thursday (April 3)

## Tweet 1 — Pain-Point Hook (Morning, ~9am)

```
Your morning routine is a lie.

You say: "I start work at 9am"

What actually happens:
9:00 — Open laptop
9:05 — Check email (17 new)
9:20 — Check Slack (4 threads to catch up on)
9:35 — Check calendar (meeting in 25 min)
9:40 — Start actual work
9:45 — Meeting

You had 5 minutes of real work.
```

**Why this works:** Relatable storytelling format, specific timeline everyone recognizes, no product pitch needed — the problem sells the solution.

## Also Post: Build-in-Public Thread

```
7 days of building CalmPilot in public.

Here's every mistake I made and what I learned:

🧵
```

Thread replies:

```
1/ Mistake: Putting links in every tweet.

The X algorithm suppresses tweets with external links. My tweets with links got 50-80 views. Without links: 200+.

Fix: Put the link in a self-reply, not the main tweet.
```

```
2/ Mistake: Posting diary updates instead of pain-point content.

"Day 5, 50 followers, $0 revenue" → 33 views
"You checked your inbox 14 times today" → 200+ views

Nobody follows your journey until they relate to your problem.
```

```
3/ Mistake: Creating content instead of distributing.

At 51 followers, 3 original tweets reach ~15 people.
One good reply on @nathanbarry's post reaches 6,900.

Below 500 followers, replies > posts. Every time.
```

```
4/ Mistake: Generic bio with vanity metrics.

"Day 5. 44 followers. $0 revenue" tells people your situation.
"AI that reads Gmail overnight and briefs you by morning" tells people your value.

Changed my bio mid-week. Profile visits doubled.
```

```
5/ What actually worked:

→ Hot takes get 3x more engagement than diary posts
→ Questions at the end of tweets force replies (13.5x algorithm weight)
→ Replying to accounts with 10K-100K followers = sweet spot
→ One demo video outperformed 10 text tweets

Week 2 starts tomorrow. Following along?
```

# Day 5 - Friday (April 4)

## Tweet 1 — Hot Take (Morning, ~9am)

```
AI assistants are useless.

ChatGPT can write a poem about your calendar but can't actually reschedule your meeting.

Claude can summarize your emails but can't reply to them.

The gap isn't intelligence. It's action.

AI that talks is a toy. AI that does is a tool.
```

**Why this works:** Controversial opener, names big brands (engagement bait), clearly positions the "AI that acts" angle that CalmPilot represents.

---

## Reply Targets

Focus on accounts posting about:
- ChatGPT / Claude / AI tools
- AI agents / autonomous AI
- Productivity tools comparison
- "What AI tool should I use?" questions

### Reply templates:

**When someone asks "what's the best AI tool for X":**
> Depends on whether you want advice or action. Most AI tools today tell you what to do. Very few actually do it. The next wave is AI that's connected to your real tools — email, calendar, Slack — and can take action, not just suggest.

**When someone demos an AI product:**
> Clean. The real test is — does it work when you're not watching? Most AI tools need you present. The ones that'll win are the ones that work overnight and brief you in the morning.

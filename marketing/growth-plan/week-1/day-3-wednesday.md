## Tweet 2 — Thread: Learnings (Afternoon, ~2pm)

```
I've been building in public for 6 days. Here's what nobody tells you about the first week:

🧵
```

**Thread replies:**

```
1/ The algorithm doesn't care about you at 50 followers.

Your tweets get shown to ~10% of your followers. That's 5 people. On a good day.

This means original content is almost useless for growth right now. You're performing for an empty room.
```

```
2/ Replies are your actual growth engine.

Every reply you leave on a big account is a free billboard. If it's good, people click your profile.

If your bio + pinned tweet are sharp, they follow.

I get more profile visits from 1 good reply than from 3 original tweets.
```

```
3/ The content that works isn't what you'd expect.

My "Day 5 numbers" post: 33 views
My "Zapier hot take": 84 views

Lesson: opinion > diary. Pain > progress. Controversy > consensus.
```

```
4/ The emotional rollercoaster is real.

Day 1: "I'm going to build an audience!"
Day 3: "Nobody cares"
Day 5: "Wait, Andrew Warner just replied to me?"

The variance is wild. One reply from the right person changes everything.
```

```
5/ What I'm changing for week 2:

→ 15+ replies/day on big accounts (up from 5)
→ More opinion/pain-point posts, fewer diary posts
→ Record a demo video of CalmPilot working
→ Stop checking analytics more than once/day

If you're building in public, what's your biggest lesson so far?
```

**Why this works:** Thread format = more time on profile. Ends with a question. Shares real data. Useful for other builders.

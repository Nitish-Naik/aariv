## Reply Targets

Focus on accounts posting about:
- No-code tools / Zapier / Make
- AI replacing workflows
- Founder frustrations with tools

### Reply templates:

**When someone praises Zapier/Make:**
> Genuine question — how long did it take you to set that up? I used Zapier for 2 years and loved it until I had to debug a Zap at 11pm because a field name changed. That's when I realized "no-code" still means "think like a developer."

**When someone talks about AI agents:**
> Most AI agents today advise but don't execute. The gap isn't intelligence — it's connection to your actual tools. An agent that can read your email but can't reply to it is just a fancy search bar.

**When someone shares a productivity hack:**
> Solid. The one that changed my workflow the most: stop checking apps manually. I built an AI that monitors Gmail/Slack/Calendar overnight and gives me one brief in the morning. Went from 40 min of catch-up to 2 min.
